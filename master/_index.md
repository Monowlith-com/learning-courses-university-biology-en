# Course overview – University · Biology {#kurseinstieg}

## Audience and prerequisites {#S1}

University-level self-study course for learners with upper-secondary school knowledge. The learning path develops relevant mathematics, chemistry and physics. It offers a broad bachelor-level core and alternative master's-level electives. It replaces neither an accredited degree nor supervised laboratory or field training.

## Scope and modules {#S2}

The course contains 144 lessons in each language across 27 modules. Learning objectives: explain biological mechanisms at molecular, cellular, organismal and ecological scales, apply quantitative models, assess scientific data and justify research decisions. M1–M16 form the bachelor-level core; M17–M18 are electives, M19 is the bachelor project. M20–M26 are alternative master-level profiles; M27 is the research-focused conclusion.
