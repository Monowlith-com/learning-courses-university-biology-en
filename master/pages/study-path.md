# Study path – Biology {#studienweg}

## Getting started {#S1}

Start with [M1-L1](../M1-L1.md) and work through the 89 core lessons (M1–M16), then select suitable electives from M17–M18 and finish with M19. Attempt the assessments before revealing the solutions. Explain the selection of independent experimental units using the example in [M1-L5](../M1-L5.md).

## Further study {#S2}

M2 already covers quantitative foundations; M3 covers natural science foundations; M4 covers cell biology and membranes; M5 covers biochemistry and bioenergetics; M6 covers molecular genetics and gene regulation; M7 covers botany, fungi and eukaryotic diversity; M8 covers zoology; M9 covers microbiology and immunobiology; M10 covers plant physiology; M11 covers animal and human physiology and behaviour; M12 covers developmental biology; M13 covers evolution and phylogenetics; M14 covers ecology and conservation; M15 covers bioinformatics; M16 covers laboratory, field and research practice and connects molecular, organismal and ecological foundations. M17 covers organismal and ecological electives; M18 covers molecular and applied electives; M19 consolidates bachelor-level research. After the core, select suitable master's profiles (M20–M26) rather than treating all as compulsory. M27 prepares for research completion. [M20-L1](../M20-L1.md) opens the available master’s elective in cell and molecular biology. [M21-L1](../M21-L1.md) opens the genomics and systems biology elective. [M22-L1](../M22-L1.md) opens the evolutionary genomics and biodiversity elective. [M23-L1](../M23-L1.md) opens the advanced ecology and environmental biology elective. [M24-L1](../M24-L1.md) opens the neuroscience and behavioural research elective. [M25-L1](../M25-L1.md) opens the microbiology and infection biology elective. [M26-L1](../M26-L1.md) opens the molecular plant sciences and biotechnology elective. [M27-L1](../M27-L1.md) opens the concluding master’s research pathway.
